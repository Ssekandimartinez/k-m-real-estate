# Asset review

Original libraries remain intact in `images/`, `screenshots/`, and `videos/`.
The video library has 119 files totaling approximately 4.97 GB. These are not bundled into the website or committed by this implementation. No verified company video was identified; large background videos would also burden mobile connections.

The original logo is a gold house and K&M lettering with the approved tagline on a dark background. The application uses a resized WebP derivative without redesigning the mark. The favicon is derived from `KM_Favicon.png`.

The supplied company-profile screenshots corroborate the overview, mission and vision. The old profile uses “Group”; the current approved brief expressly overrides that wording. Existing website screenshots inform large photography, restrained navigation and editorial spacing; no template code or imagery has been copied.

Selected existing photographs are used only as illustrative architecture, interior and service imagery. They are not seeded as active listings, completed projects or testimonials. Ownership and reuse permissions for supplied media should be confirmed by the company before publication, especially files with third-party business names or watermarks.

Optimized public derivatives are generated by `scripts/prepare-assets.mjs`; original assets are read-only inputs. `docs/asset-manifest.json` records their sources.

Design references reviewed: Pam Golding (photography and clear property specifications), Seeff (sale/rent navigation), HassConsult (corporate presentation), Property24 (filters), BuyRentKenya (category selection and mobile search). The Broll URL timed out during review. K&M uses its own Burgundy & Sandstone styling and supplied company content.
